The team

Founded by three practising doctors

NavigoSurg is a clinician-led company. Our founders are three practising doctors — two radiologists and a medical oncologist — who work daily with the scans, the surgeons and the decisions this software is built to support. That perspective shapes what we build and how carefully we make claims about it.
